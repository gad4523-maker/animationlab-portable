# Staging and look

## Shot intent and style

Pin references to jobs such as pose design, atmosphere, or edit rhythm. Record silhouette traits, body proportions, contour policy, shadow thresholds, and the light direction. Make one clean face, one body silhouette, and one neutral patch the look match across shots. Check actual rendered colors in BT.709 and compare a shared reference swatch when changing engines. Choose whether a material is lit, unlit, or emissive before adding grading. Preserve the existing asset policy.

Use separate foreground, action, and background depth. Give the eye one destination using value separation and local contrast. Keep eyes, hands, target contact, and subtitles away from the active UI guides by checking the final 9:16 framing. The lab reserves a 12.5% right rail and lower 22.5% as internal guides. Check a destination preview when available.

## Camera and lighting

- 2D. Place the action on a stable plane and offset foreground and background travel. Track the leading joint or point of contact. Check intersections between sprite layers.
- 2.5D. Use the render camera to check parallax on the closest and farthest objects. For character cards, test the orbit at both ends so a drawing does not become edge-on. Use anchored occluders to show depth.
- 3D. Start with an angle that shows the gaze, support foot, and action direction. Change one dominant camera move at a time. Use lens, framing, and distance for different purposes. Preserve screen direction across cuts unless a frontal or neutral view resets the axis.

Stage a key, a softer fill, and a rim only where needed for the silhouette. Tie contact shadows to supporting surfaces. Use glow and lens effects around an emissive source and check whether they bury eyes or hand shapes. Check the black level and warm/cool balance on both sides of a cut.

## Assess a candidate renderer

Test a small crop with the actual art and camera. The candidate must preserve required deformation, layer boundaries and the shot's most extreme pose. If the engine is unavailable, redesign the shot around an implementation you can render and recheck the look against adjacent shots. Estimate cost using the actual scene at its target profile.
