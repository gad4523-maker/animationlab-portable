# Quality pass

Do one focused draft review at typical phone scale. Include thumbnails for the opening, prep, release, impact, and recovery. Inspect neighboring frames when any silhouette, matte, contact, or timing looks suspect. Review the master once with sound. Compare like beats with the existing procedural clips.

- Character. Check elbow and knee bends, foot lock, silhouette, hand contact, eye line, facial readability, and secondary motion.
- Picture. Check screen direction, whether the subject and target share the frame when needed, subtitle and rail clearance, visible asset or shader glitches, and continuity of light and palette through cuts.
- Motion and mix. Check frame count, cadence and transition overlaps, cue alignment, true peak after AAC, and full decode. A motion gate finds candidate defects and has limited ability to judge shot quality.
